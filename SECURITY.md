# Security

## Reporting a vulnerability

Email **support@emodely.com** with "Security" in the subject line. Do not open a public issue.

Please include:

- what you found and where (this plugin, or the engine service at engine.emodely.com);
- the steps to reproduce it;
- the impact you expect.

We will confirm that we received your report and keep you informed while we fix it.

## Scope

- **In scope.**
  - The plugin files in this repository.
  - The eModely Engine service at engine.emodely.com, including sign-in, access control, uploads and download links.
- **Out of scope.** Claude and its apps. Report those to Anthropic.

## Rules for testing

- Test only with your own account and your own data. Do not access, change or delete anyone else's data.
- Do not degrade the service. No denial-of-service, load testing or automated scanning at volume.
- Do not try to extract the engine, its chiller curves or its coefficients. The [Terms of Use](https://engine.emodely.com/terms) forbid this, including systematic series of simulations.
- Give us reasonable time to fix an issue before you disclose it.

## If your account or API key is compromised

Email support@emodely.com straight away. We can revoke API keys and suspend accounts.
